At a Glance

Key Takeaways

Performance

Revenue for veterinary services in Hungary is estimated at 75.0 billion forints in 2026, growing at an annualised 11.1% over the past five years. Domestic demand, the forint exchange rate and energy costs have been the main swing factors.

Over the next five years, revenue is forecast to expand at 3.7% a year. Employment is expected to fall as operators invest in productivity amid a tight Hungarian labour market.

EU funding, wage growth and Hungary's position within central European supply chains will shape profit margins, which currently stand at 20.9% of revenue.

Products and Markets

The largest product segment is consulting, followed by smaller specialised lines. Buyers are increasingly price-sensitive as consumer price inflation eases only slowly.

Exports to the EU, mainly Germany and neighbouring countries, remain an important outlet, while domestic households and businesses account for the rest of demand.
